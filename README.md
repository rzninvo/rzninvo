I'm a master's student in AI at UZH, doing research in ETH Zurich's [Computer Vision and Geometry Group](https://cvg.ethz.ch/) with Daniel Barath, and leading the computer vision team at microagi.

With Mahbod Tajdini, I co-develop [microSLAM](https://www.microagi.ai/articles/microslam), dense monocular SLAM for dynamic egocentric scenes. It runs in production and ranked first among monocular-only entries on all five [LaMAria](https://www.lamaria.ethz.ch/leaderboard) challenges (Sep 2026).

Our ECCV 2026 paper, [LangLoc](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05077), finds where you are in a known indoor scene from just a description of what you see ([code](https://github.com/rzninvo/Lang-Loc)).
